ITR Section234B -
 req 1 ) If the taxpayer fails to pay advance tax or if the advance tax paid is less than 90% of the
assessed tax, a simple interest of 1% per month (or part of a month) is levied on the shortfall amount. If a user has a
tax liability (after TDS) of more than ₹10,000, they are required to pay Advance Tax. If they pay less than 90% of the
required tax by the end of the financial year (March 31st), they must pay a penalty of 1% interest per month starting
from April 1st of the Assessment Year.

for ex -
       Total Tax Liability = 1,00,000
       TDS Deducted = 10,000
       Assessed Tax (Net) = 90,000
       90% Threshold = 81,000 (User must have paid at least this much to avoid 234B)
       Advance Tax Paid = 50,000 (Since 50k < 81k, Section 234B applies)
       Shortfall Amount = 90,000 - 50,000 = 40,000
       Payment Date = December 12th
       Delay Period = April 1st to December 12th
       Duration in months = 9 months (Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sept, Oct, Nov, Dec)
       Interest u\s 234B = 40,000 * 0.01 * 9

ITR section 234C-
            ==================================================================
                          SECTION 234C INTEREST CALCULATION LOGIC
            ==================================================================

            LOGIC SUMMARY:
            1. APPLICABILITY:
               - Applies if the Tax Liability (Assessed Tax - TDS) is Rs 10,000 or more.
               - Applies if Advance Tax is not paid in accordance with the specified installments.

            2. INSTALLMENT SCHEDULE & BUFFERS (For Non-44AD/ADA Cases):
               Section 234C calculates interest on the shortfall of cumulative tax paid
               by specific deadline dates.

               | DATE        | TARGET % | BUFFER % | PERIOD   |
               |-------------|----------|----------|----------|
               | 15th June   | 15%      | 12%      | 3 Months |
               | 15th Sept   | 45%      | 36%      | 3 Months |
               | 15th Dec    | 75%      | 75%      | 3 Months |
               | 15th Mar    | 100%     | 100%     | 1 Month  |

               * Buffer Rule: For June and Sept, if you pay at least the "Buffer %",
                 no interest is charged for that quarter. If you pay less, interest
                 is calculated on the FULL Target %.

            3. CALCULATION FORMULA:
               Shortfall = (Assessed Tax * Target %) - (Cumulative Tax Paid by Date)
               Interest  = Shortfall * 1% * Period (3 months or 1 month)

            ==================================================================
                                 VALIDATION EXAMPLES
            ==================================================================

            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            EXAMPLE 1: THE "JUST MISSED" BUFFER CASE
            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            Scenario:
            - Total Tax Liability:   100,000
            - TDS Deducted:          0
            - Net Tax Payable:       100,000

            Payment History:
            - 12 June:  11,000 (11%) -> Missed 12% buffer.
            - 12 Sept:  25,000 (Total 36k -> 36%) -> Hit 36% buffer exactly.
            - 12 Dec:   30,000 (Total 66k -> 66%) -> Missed 75% target.
            - 12 Mar:   34,000 (Total 100k -> 100%) -> Hit 100% target.

            Calculation Trace:
            1. JUNE 15 (Target 15% = 15,000)
               - Paid: 11,000
               - Check Buffer: 11,000 < 12,000 (12% of 100k). Buffer missed.
               - Shortfall: 15,000 - 11,000 = 4,000
               - Interest: 4,000 * 1% * 3 months = 120

            2. SEPT 15 (Target 45% = 45,000)
               - Paid Cumulative: 11k + 25k = 36,000
               - Check Buffer: 36,000 >= 36,000 (36% of 100k). Buffer met.
               - Interest: 0

            3. DEC 15 (Target 75% = 75,000)
               - Paid Cumulative: 36k + 30k = 66,000
               - Check Target: 66,000 < 75,000. No buffer allowed.
               - Shortfall: 75,000 - 66,000 = 9,000
               - Interest: 9,000 * 1% * 3 months = 270

            4. MAR 15 (Target 100% = 100,000)
               - Paid Cumulative: 66k + 34k = 100,000
               - Shortfall: 0
               - Interest: 0

            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            TOTAL INTEREST 234C:                                  120 + 270 = 390
            ------------------------------------------------------------------


            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            EXAMPLE 2: THE "LATE BLOOMER" (PAID EVERYTHING IN MARCH)
            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            Scenario:
            - Total Tax Liability:   200,000
            - Paid on 15 June:       0
            - Paid on 15 Sept:       0
            - Paid on 15 Dec:        0
            - Paid on 15 Mar:        200,000

            Calculation Trace:
            1. JUNE 15 (Target 15% = 30,000)
               - Paid: 0
               - Shortfall: 30,000
               - Interest: 30,000 * 1% * 3 months = 900

            2. SEPT 15 (Target 45% = 90,000)
               - Paid: 0
               - Shortfall: 90,000
               - Interest: 90,000 * 1% * 3 months = 2,700

            3. DEC 15 (Target 75% = 150,000)
               - Paid: 0
               - Shortfall: 150,000
               - Interest: 150,000 * 1% * 3 months = 4,500

            4. MAR 15 (Target 100% = 200,000)
               - Paid: 200,000 (Paid on due date)
               - Shortfall: 0
               - Interest: 0

            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            TOTAL INTEREST 234C:                      900 + 2700 + 4500 = 8,100
            ------------------------------------------------------------------


            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            EXAMPLE 3: PRESUMPTIVE TAXATION (SECTION 44AD/44ADA) CASE
            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            Note: Taxpayers opting for Presumptive Scheme only need to pay
            100% by March 15th. They are exempt from June, Sept, Dec installments.

            Scenario:
            - Total Tax Liability: 50,000
            - Paid on 14 Mar:      40,000
            - Paid on 31 Mar:      10,000

            Calculation Trace:
            1. JUNE / SEPT / DEC: Not Applicable (Interest = 0)

            2. MAR 15 (Target 100% = 50,000)
               - Paid: 40,000
               - Shortfall: 50,000 - 40,000 = 10,000
               - Interest: 10,000 * 1% * 1 month = 100

            ------------------------------------------------------------------
            TOTAL INTEREST 234C:                                            100
            ------------------------------------------------------------------
